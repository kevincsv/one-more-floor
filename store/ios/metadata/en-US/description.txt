Climb. Don't look down.

One More Floor Escape is an endless 8-bit climb up a tower that builds itself as you go. The camera only moves up and keeps pushing: fall below the screen and the run is over.

STORY MODE: LA HUIDA
You wake inside a living tower that wants you dead, and every new rule is one of its attacks. You don't know why. You only know you have to climb. Cross the five biomes, beat a boss at the end of each one, find notes left by other climbers and remember who you came here to find. Two endings, checkpoints after every boss, and no checkpoints at all on Hardcore.

NEW RULES EVERY 15 SECONDS
Darkness, wind, ice, mirrored controls, invisible enemies... A new modifier joins the run and the oldest one leaves. Skulls tell you how nasty each one is, and two rules together can make a new combo.

LEVEL UP AS YOU CLIMB
Enemies drop experience. Every level up gives you three cards: take a new bonus or throw away a bad modifier.

FIVE BIOMES
The Forgotten Castle, the Living Depths, the Machine Kingdom, the Void and the Unknown, each with its own enemies and chiptune music.

SECRET ROOMS
Hit cracked walls to find treasure, a merchant and risky pacts.

MORE TO PLAY
• Four heroes and unlockable looks
• Daily challenge: the same tower for everyone, once a day
• Easy mode with an extra heart and a slower camera, with its own records
• Achievements and a codex of every rule and bonus
• Touch controls built for phones: D-pad, invisible stick or classic layout

FREE TO CLIMB
The endless climb is free. One optional purchase, Full Escape, unlocks Horde mode, Kai, Nyx, Brisa and every look, for good. Horde mode also unlocks for free when you finish La Huida on Normal or Hardcore. No ads, no accounts, no tracking, and it plays offline.
