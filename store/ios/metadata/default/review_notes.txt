Single-player offline game. No account, no login, one optional non-consumable in-app purchase (Full Escape: unlocks Horde mode, Kai, Nyx and all looks, with a Restore Purchases button), no ads and no data collection. Controls: on-screen D-pad, HIT and JUMP buttons (other layouts in Settings). Tap the title screen, then CLIMB to start a run. An EASY toggle on the title screen gives an extra heart and a slower camera if you want to see more of the game quickly.
