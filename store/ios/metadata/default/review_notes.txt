APP OVERVIEW
One More Floor Escape is a single-player 8-bit platformer. You climb an endless tower while new rules (modifiers) switch on every 15 seconds. Audience: casual and arcade players, rated 9+ (mild cartoon fantasy violence). Everything except the duel works fully offline.

SCREEN RECORDING
A screen recording made on a physical iPhone is attached in App Review Information. It shows launch, the main modes, the Full Escape purchase and restore, Game Center and iCloud.

NO ACCOUNT, NO LOGIN
The app has no account creation and no login, so no demo account is needed. There is no user-generated content, no chat and no social features. It shows no ads and collects no data (App Privacy: Data Not Collected).

HOW TO REACH EACH FEATURE
1. Launch the app and tap the title screen.
2. Pick a difficulty (EASY, NORMAL or HARDCORE). EASY is the quickest way to see a lot of the game.
3. ESCAPE: the main endless climb, free.
4. HISTORIA / STORY ("La Huida"): story mode with five biomes, a boss at the end of each, collectible notes, checkpoints and two endings, free.
5. HORDA / HORDE: locked by default. It unlocks for free by finishing story mode on Normal or Hardcore, or right away with the Full Escape purchase. Tapping it while locked explains this.
6. DUELO / DUEL: a live race between two devices. On device A open DUEL and create a room; it shows a 4-character code (letters and digits). On device B open DUEL, enter that code, and both press READY. A small relay server only forwards the two players' positions in real time and stores nothing. There are no accounts and no names, and only the chosen hero is sent.
7. SHOP: a cosmetic skin shop that uses in-game coins only, with no real money.
8. Bottom tabs: FEATS (achievements), RECORDS/RANKS (Game Center leaderboards) and SETTINGS.
Controls: an on-screen D-pad plus HIT and JUMP buttons. Other layouts are in Settings.

IN-APP PURCHASE (non-consumable, com.onemorefloor.escape.full, "Full Escape")
- To buy: tap the FULL ESCAPE button on the title screen, or tap the locked HORDE button, then confirm with the Apple payment sheet.
- It unlocks the Horde mode, the heroes Kai, Nyx and Brisa, and every non-shop look for good. Everything it unlocks can also be earned by playing.
- To restore: SETTINGS > RESTORE PURCHASE.
- It can be tested with a Sandbox account.

APPLE SERVICES
- Game Center: optional, for achievements and leaderboards. If you are not signed in, the game still plays normally.
- iCloud (key-value storage): progress is backed up and restored automatically. SETTINGS shows the iCloud status and offers RESTORE FROM ICLOUD when a copy exists.
- No third-party SDKs, ads or analytics. The only network use is the duel relay above, which forwards gameplay positions in real time and keeps nothing.

REGIONS
The content is the same in every region. The app is in English and Spanish and follows the device language.

REGULATED CONTENT
None. There is no gambling, real-money prizes, health, finance or regulated industry content.
